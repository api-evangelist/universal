Universal Corporation is the world's leading global leaf tobacco merchant and processor. Operating in approximately 30 countries across Africa, Asia, Europe, and the Americas, Universal selects, buys, processes, packs, stores, ships, and finances leaf tobacco for sale to manufacturers of consumer tobacco products. The company has developed digital platforms including MobiLeaf, a tablet-based grower management system for tobacco farmer support and crop tracking.
